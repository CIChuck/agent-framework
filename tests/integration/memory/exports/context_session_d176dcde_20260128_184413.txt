### Our Rich History The Harrisburg Regional Chamber was founded in 1884 as the Harrisburg Board of Trade. In 1913, it was decided that the organization should be formally incorporated as a Chamber of Commerce and by 1914 that official designation was given to the organization. CREDC was founded in 1983 and merged with the Chamber in 1994.

Together with our members, we’ve accomplished some pretty amazing things over the last 140 years. Here are just a few:

* Beautifying Harrisburg through the City Beautiful Movement (1900s)

* Bringing reliable, modern electricity to the city of Harrisburg (1920s)

* Building the region’s first airport (Today’s Capital City Airport, 1930s)

* Converting Olmsted Airforce Base into Harrisburg International Airport (1960s)

* Forming Penn State Capital Campus (Today’s Penn State Harrisburg, 1960s)

* Creating the Hershey Center for Applied Research (2000s)

* Secured $500 M to repair the I-83 bridge– largest federal grant ever given to a transportation project in PA (2024)
